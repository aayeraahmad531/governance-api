Project 01

AI Hiring Bias Detector

Reads a job posting and underlines the language that quietly screens people out — with a concrete replacement for every flag.

StackFastAPI · LangChain · Gemini
GroundingCurated bias lexicon
DeployDocker · Cloud Run
The problem

I reviewed a stack of engineering job descriptions at work and found them loaded with gendered phrasing and age assumptions — "rockstar ninja," "digital native," fifteen years of experience with a three-year-old framework. Nobody writes that on purpose. They copy a template, and qualified people quietly don't apply.

The hard part isn't detection, it's consistency. Ask a model to find bias freehand and it flags different things every run, at different confidence. So the flags here are matched against a curated lexicon with research-backed replacements — the model's job is locating and explaining, not deciding what counts.

How it works
01

Scan

The posting is matched against an indexed lexicon of terms with category, severity and a suggested replacement.

02

Retrieve

Matching entries are pulled by vector similarity, so near-misses and variants are caught, not just exact strings.

03

Locate

The model quotes each offending span verbatim so the frontend can underline it in the original text.

04

Score

Per-category confidence and an overall bias score, with the replacement drawn from the retrieved entry.

Every span is quoted verbatim from your input — that's what lets the underlines land on the exact words rather than approximate positions. Responses are validated against a Pydantic schema before they leave the service, so a malformed model output becomes a retry, never a broken page.
