Project 02

EU AI Act Compliance Checker

Audits an AI system against six principles from the Act — and cites only articles it actually retrieved, never one recalled from memory.

StackFastAPI · LangChain · Gemini
Corpus298 chunks · Art. 1–113
Version02024R1689-20260727
The problem

I spent too many hours reading EU AI Act PDFs while reviewing AI features at work, looking up the same article numbers over and over. The obvious fix is to ask a model — but models get article numbers wrong, confidently and often. A compliance tool that invents a citation is worse than no tool at all.

So this one never asks the model to remember. The consolidated Act is chunked and embedded, the relevant articles are retrieved first, and any citation the model produces that isn't in the retrieved set gets dropped before it reaches you. The retrieved passage is shown under every violation, so you can check the judgement against the text yourself.

How it works
01

Embed

Your description is embedded locally with MiniLM — no network call, sub-millisecond.

02

Retrieve

A dot product across 298 indexed Act chunks returns the eight most relevant articles with their metadata.

03

Evaluate

The model assesses six principles using only the retrieved text as its source of law.

04

Verify

Citations outside the retrieved set are dropped and counted. What survives is traceable.

The six principles: transparency, human oversight, accuracy and robustness, non-discrimination, privacy, accountability.

Try it

Describe a system and see what the Act says.

The sample is a classic high-risk case. Swap in something from your own product.

POST /api/compliancesample mode

Your text is sent to Google Gemini and may be used to improve their models. Nothing is logged or stored here. Don't paste confidential system details.

Under the hood

{ "content": "...", "context": "..." }

200
{
  "compliant": false,
  "score": 50,
  "violations": [
    { "principle": "Human oversight",
      "severity": "high",
      "article_reference": "Art. 14",
      "description": "...",
      "action": "...",
      "source": "<the retrieved passage itself>" }
  ],
  "summary": "..."
}

The source field is the point of the whole design. Every violation carries the text it was derived from, so a claim about Article 14 can be checked without leaving the page.
