What I actually do

Four years at Harman, most of it circling one question: how do you know a model is wrong before your users do?

That question has taken me through RAG pipelines, structured outputs, evaluation across model families, and a great deal of prompt archaeology. I build the services that sit around models — FastAPI, Docker, Cloud Run — and I care most about the parts that make an answer checkable rather than merely fluent. Google-certified in Generative AI, Vertex AI, Agentic AI and Responsible AI, though the certificates matter less than the habit of asking what a system does when it fails.
